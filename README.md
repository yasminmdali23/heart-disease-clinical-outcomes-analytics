# 🫀 Interactive Heart Disease & Clinical Outcomes Analytics Dashboard

A clinical analytics Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate heart disease patient survival rates, key serum biomarkers, and comorbidity risk profiles across diverse age and demographic groups.

---

## 📌 Key Clinical Insights
* **Survival Rate Tracking:** Monitored a **67.89%** overall survival rate (**203 alive vs. 96 deceased**) for high-level clinical outcomes.
* **Comorbidity & Risk Factor Analysis:** Cross-analyzed patient survival against concurrent conditions including cancer and diabetes across age cohorts.
* **Biomarker Correlations:** Evaluated serum measurements and trends against age demographics to identify critical risk indicators.
* **Interactive Healthcare UI:** Dark-mode dashboard featuring gender slicers, age-group drill-downs, and clinical asset formatting.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills
* **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
* **Skills:** Healthcare Analytics, Clinical Data Analysis, KPI Tracking, Data Visualization
